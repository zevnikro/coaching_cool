Common Questions

Will you tell me what to invest in? +

No. I'm not a licensed financial advisor and I won't give investment advice. What I will do is help you build the habits, clarity, and confidence to make your own informed decisions — or to work with a financial advisor more effectively.

Do I need to share my exact financial details? +

Only what you're comfortable sharing. Some clients share numbers openly because it helps, others work in broad strokes. There's no spreadsheet audit — this is about your relationship with money, not your bank balance.

I'm in debt. Is coaching still right for me? +

Absolutely. Many clients come with debt. Coaching helps you create a realistic strategy AND deal with the shame and avoidance that often keeps people stuck in the debt cycle.

Can I do this as a couple? +

Yes! Money conversations between partners can be transformative when there's a neutral third person in the room. Couples sessions follow the same pricing — you both join the same call.

How is this different from reading a finance book? +

Books give you information. Coaching gives you transformation. The gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it is where most people get stuck. That gap is exactly where coaching lives.
