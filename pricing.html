Common questions

What happens after my first $29.99 session? +

Nothing automatic. After your first session, you decide if you want to continue. If you do, pick either Easy Jive or All In. No pressure, no auto-billing surprises.

What's the difference between monthly and 10-month? +

Monthly plans bill every month and you can cancel anytime. The 10-month option is a one-time upfront payment that saves you up to $991 — it's the best per-session value if you're committed to the journey.

What currency will I be charged in? +

Prices are listed in USD. Stripe automatically converts to your local currency at checkout, so you'll see the amount in EUR, GBP, or whatever your card uses.

How do I cancel my monthly subscription? +

Just send me a message or email. I'll cancel it immediately — no questions asked, no retention tricks. You can also manage it through the Stripe customer portal.

Can I switch between Easy Jive and All In? +

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. The change takes effect on your next billing cycle.

Is this therapy? +

No. Coaching is forward-focused — we work on where you want to go, not diagnose where you've been. If therapy is more appropriate, I'll tell you honestly.
