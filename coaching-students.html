Common Questions

Is this like career counseling? +

Not exactly. We go deeper than career advice — we work on clarity, confidence, and the habits that drive everything else.

Do I need to have a specific problem? +

Not at all. Many students come in feeling "stuck" without knowing exactly why. You don't need a crisis to benefit from coaching.

Do my parents need to be involved? +

Nope. This is your space. Everything is confidential between us.

How are sessions done? +

All sessions are via Zoom video call. 60 minutes each. Flexible scheduling that works around your life.
