Common Questions

Is this therapy? +

No. Coaching is forward-focused — we work on where you want to go, not diagnose where you've been. If therapy is more appropriate, I'll tell you honestly.

Do I need to have a specific problem? +

Not at all. Many dads come in simply wanting to be more intentional. You don't need a crisis to benefit from coaching.

What if my partner doesn't understand? +

That's more common than you'd think. Coaching is for YOU — and the ripple effects on your relationships tend to speak for themselves.

How are sessions done? +

All sessions are via Zoom video call. 60 minutes each. Flexible scheduling that works around your life.
