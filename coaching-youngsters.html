Common Questions

Is this just for people with problems? +

Nah. It's for anyone who wants to level up. Think of it as having a really smart friend who asks the right questions.

Do I need to have a specific problem? +

Not at all. Most young adults come in wanting 'something more' — that's enough to start. You don't need a crisis to benefit from coaching.

What if I don't know what to talk about? +

That's totally fine. I'll guide the conversation. You don't need to show up with a plan.

How are sessions done? +

All sessions are via Zoom video call. 60 minutes each. Flexible scheduling that works around your life.
